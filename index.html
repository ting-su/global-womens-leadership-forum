GOOD TO KNOW

Before we meet.

Who is the first forum for?＋

Women founders around the world and women students who dream of founding. The forum is an opportunity to learn, connect and exchange perspectives across generations and experiences.

When will registration open?＋

The first forum is coming soon. The date, time zone, final programme and registration details will be announced here. For now, you can prepare a personal interest note; it stays with you and is not a registration.

Are online forums and private gatherings the same?＋

They are separate experiences. Online forums bring a global audience into conversation. Closed-door gatherings in Menlo Park are smaller, curated spaces with sharing boundaries agreed in advance.

Who hosts LikeLight?＋

The community is hosted by Ting Su, in Menlo Park and online. Amber S hosts our first online forum.

Privacy & participation

LikeLight's first forum is in preparation. Dates, speakers, registration and any fees will be confirmed before enrollment opens. The programme shown here is a proposed outline.

The interest form generates a text file in your browser. Your entries are not uploaded, emailed or saved by this website, and are cleared when the dialog closes. No member account, payment or public-comment service is active.

This site does not configure analytics or advertising cookies. GitHub Pages and external font providers may process normal request metadata. Official contact details and registration privacy information will be published when registration opens.

Private gatherings are separate from online forums. Participants will agree recording, attribution and sharing boundaries in advance. No private attendee lists are published here.
